# Helios Health Clinic Operations

## Opening hours

Helios clinics open from 07:30 to 19:00 on weekdays and from 09:00 to 13:00 on Saturdays. Clinics are closed on Sundays.

## Hotel bookings for visiting consultants

Visiting consultants may book hotels up to 310 GBP per night in London.

## Patient records

Patient records are kept for 8 years after the last appointment.
