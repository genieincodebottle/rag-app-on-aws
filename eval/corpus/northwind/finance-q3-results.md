# Q3 Financial Results (Restricted: Finance)

## Summary

Q3 revenue was 48.2 million GBP, up 11 percent on Q2. Gross margin was 41.5 percent. The X300 product line contributed 19.7 million GBP of revenue.

## Outlook

The board approved a hiring plan of 35 additional engineers for Q4. Full-year revenue guidance is unchanged at 185 million GBP.
