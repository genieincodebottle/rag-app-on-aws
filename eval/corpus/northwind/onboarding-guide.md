# New Starter Onboarding Guide

## Before your first day

Your manager raises a laptop request in the IT service portal at least five working days before you start. The laptop is delivered to the office reception on your first morning.

## First week

On day one you meet your onboarding buddy, a colleague from another team who answers day-to-day questions for your first three months. Complete the mandatory security awareness course by the end of your first week.

## First month

Agree your objectives with your manager within your first 30 days. Your probation period is six months, with a review meeting at the end of month three.
