# Travel Policy 2025

Effective 1 January 2025. Applies to all Northwind Robotics employees travelling on company business.

## Booking

All travel must be booked through the company travel portal at least 14 days before departure. Bookings made outside the portal are not reimbursed unless the portal had no availability.

## Hotels

The nightly hotel limit is 200 GBP in London and 150 GBP in all other UK cities. Outside the UK the limit is 180 USD per night. A stay above the limit needs written approval from a finance manager before the booking is made.

## Meals

The daily meal allowance is 55 GBP, including tips. Alcohol is not reimbursed.

## Flights

Economy class is required for flights under six hours. Premium economy is allowed for flights of six hours or longer. Business class needs director approval.
