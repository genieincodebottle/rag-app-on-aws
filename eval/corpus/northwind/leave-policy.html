Leave Policy

Annual leave

Full-time employees receive 25 days of annual leave per year, plus public holidays. Part-time employees receive leave in proportion to their contracted hours.

Carrying over leave

Up to 5 unused days can be carried into the next year. Carried-over days must be used by 31 March or they are lost.

Parental leave

Employees with at least six months of service receive 20 weeks of parental leave at full pay, followed by up to 12 further weeks at half pay.

Sick leave

Tell your manager before 10:00 on the first day of sickness. A doctor's note is needed for absences longer than seven calendar days.
