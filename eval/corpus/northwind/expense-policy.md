# Expense Policy

## Submitting expenses

Submit every expense claim in the finance system within 30 days of the purchase date. Claims submitted after 90 days are rejected. Attach an itemised receipt for any single item over 25 GBP.

## Approval limits

Your line manager approves claims up to 500 GBP. Claims from 500 GBP to 5,000 GBP need director approval. Claims above 5,000 GBP need approval from the Chief Financial Officer.

## Corporate cards

Corporate cards are issued to employees who travel more than six times a year. Personal purchases on a corporate card must be repaid within 14 days and are reported to the employee's manager.

## Reimbursement

Approved claims are paid with the next monthly payroll run. Claims approved after the 20th of the month are paid in the following month.
