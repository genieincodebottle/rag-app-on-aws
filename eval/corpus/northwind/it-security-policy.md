# Information Security Policy

## Passwords and sign-in

Passwords must be at least 14 characters long. Passwords are changed only after a suspected compromise, not on a fixed schedule. Multi-factor authentication is mandatory for email, the VPN and every system that holds customer data.

## Laptops

Company laptops use full-disk encryption and lock automatically after 5 minutes of inactivity. Do not store customer data on USB drives.

## Reporting incidents

Report a suspected security incident to security@northwind.example within one hour of noticing it. Do not try to investigate the incident yourself and do not delete evidence such as suspicious emails.

## Access reviews

Managers review the system access of their team every quarter. Access for people who leave the company is removed on their last working day.
