# X200 Robot Arm Technical Specification

## Mechanical

The X200 has six axes, a maximum payload of 12 kg and a reach of 1.3 m. Position repeatability is plus or minus 0.02 mm. The arm weighs 54 kg.

## Environment

The X200 is rated IP54. It operates between 0 and 45 degrees Celsius at up to 85 percent relative humidity, non-condensing.

## Power

The controller needs a 230 V single-phase supply and draws 1.2 kW at full load.

## Maintenance

Replace the joint lubricant every 20,000 operating hours. Recalibrate the arm after any collision or after moving it to a new mounting surface.
