# Travel Policy 2026

Effective 1 January 2026. This policy replaces the Travel Policy 2025 in full.

## Booking

Book all travel through the company travel portal at least 10 days before departure. Last-minute travel for a customer emergency may be booked directly and must be logged in the portal within two working days.

## Hotels

The nightly hotel limit is 240 GBP in London and 160 GBP in all other UK cities. Outside the UK the limit is 200 USD per night. A stay above the limit needs approval from a finance manager before the booking is made.

## Meals

The daily meal allowance is 60 GBP, including tips. Alcohol is not reimbursed.

## Flights

Economy class is required for flights under six hours. Premium economy is allowed for flights of six hours or longer. Business class needs director approval and is only available on flights over ten hours.

## Rail

Standard class rail is required for journeys under two hours. First class is allowed for journeys of two hours or more when booked at least seven days in advance.
