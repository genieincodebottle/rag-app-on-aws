# X300 Robot Arm Technical Specification

## Mechanical

The X300 has six axes, a maximum payload of 25 kg and a reach of 1.8 m. Position repeatability is plus or minus 0.05 mm. The arm weighs 118 kg.

## Environment

The X300 is rated IP67 and can be washed down. It operates between 5 and 50 degrees Celsius at up to 95 percent relative humidity, non-condensing.

## Power

The controller needs a 400 V three-phase supply and draws 3.5 kW at full load.

## Maintenance

Replace the joint lubricant every 15,000 operating hours. Recalibrate the arm after any collision or after moving it to a new mounting surface.
